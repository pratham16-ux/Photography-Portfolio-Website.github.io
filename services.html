Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 3–6 months ahead for weddings and 2–3 weeks for portraits and events to secure your preferred date.

Do you travel outside Bengaluru?

Yes — we shoot destination weddings and pre-wedding sessions across India, with travel costs quoted separately.

How long until we receive our photos?

Curated highlight galleries are delivered in 5–7 days; full edited galleries within 10–14 days.

Can we customise a package?

Absolutely. Every package can be tailored with add-ons like drone coverage, albums, or a second shooter.

What is the advance payment?

A 30% advance confirms your booking, with the balance due before final delivery.
